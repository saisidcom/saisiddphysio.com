About Sai Sidd

Where Physiotherapy Excellence Begins

At Sai Sidd College of Physiotherapy, students learn the deep science behind human movement and rehabilitation while mastering practical clinical skills needed for professional leadership.

We connect rigorous theoretical understanding with patient-centered practical care. Our institution prepares healthcare practitioners to restore functional mobility, rehabilitate acute trauma, and elevate patient quality of life.

Featured Degree Programme

Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT)

A comprehensive 4.5-year programme combining academic knowledge, practical lab training, and hospital rotations to prepare students for a rewarding healthcare career.

Year 1: Human Anatomy & Basic Medical Sciences

Establishing core concepts of human biology, musculoskeletal anatomy, physiology, and biochemistry necessary for understanding human body mechanics.

  • Human Anatomy & Histology
  • Physiology & Biochemistry
  • Basic Biomechanics
